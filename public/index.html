Extra Steam tools that use the same sign-in. Switch on only what you use: a tool that's off isn't loaded and uses no resources.


		
		

			
📷 Screenshot uploader
				

			
Take screenshots in your games as usual (F12 in Steam). When you close the game, the new ones are uploaded to your Steam screenshot library on their own, set to Private unless you choose otherwise. Keep Kettle running in the tray.

🗂 Screenshot manager
				

			
All your Steam screenshots in one gallery. Like or dislike them, back up the ones you like to a folder, and move the ones you don't into a trash you can restore from. You can also import any image, resized to fit Steam, to upload it as a screenshot.

🚀 Launch options
				

			
Keep several launch option configs per game, for example "Performance" or "Streaming", next to its Default (what Steam had when you first opened the game here), and switch with one click. Steam only reads launch options when it starts, so a change is saved while Steam is closed: either now (Steam restarts) or as soon as you close it.

			

			

				

				

					

						
						
					

					

				

				

					

					

					

					

						
						
					

				

				

				

			

		


		
		

			
🏆 Achievement manager
				

			
See every achievement of a game and unlock or lock them, like Steam Achievement Manager. The game doesn't have to be installed or running.

🏷 Wishlist sale alerts
				

			
Checks your wishlist every few hours and notifies you when a game goes on sale, drops to the price you set for it, or (optional) may be delisted soon. Each one is announced once.

			

				

				

					

						
						
					

					
					
					

						
						

				

				

			

		


		
		

			
🎁 Sale event helper
				

			
During Steam sales: claims the free daily sticker and goes through your Discovery Queue for the event rewards, once a day. Outside a sale it just finds nothing to do.

			

				

				

					
					
					

				

				

			

		


		
		

			
🆓 Free game claimer
				

			
Paste store links or AppIDs of free games (free-to-play, or free to keep for a limited time) and they're added to your account. Several at once is fine.

			

				

					
					

						

				

				

			

		


		
		

			
🃏 Badges & boosters
				

			
Your badge progress per game, a notification when a badge can be crafted, and the booster packs you can make with gems. Read-only: it never crafts or spends gems.

			

				

				

					
					
					

						My badges ↗
						

				

				

				

			

		


		
		

			
📊 Playtime reports
				

			
A day-by-day history of what you played (idled hours count too, the way Steam counts them), a weekly summary every Monday, and games from your library worth a try. The history starts when you switch it on.

			

				

				

					
				

				

				

				

				
Worth a try

				

			

		


		
		

			
🧹 Library cleanup
				

			
Games you never played or barely touched, games you can still refund (bought in the last 14 days, played under 2 hours), and DLC you own for games you don't. Worked out when you open it; nothing runs in the background.

💰 Market value
				

			
What your trading cards, foils, backgrounds, emoticons and boosters are worth on the Steam Market (lowest listing). Prices are looked up slowly, about one item every few seconds, because Steam limits this; they're kept for a day. Read-only: nothing is listed or sold.

			

				

				

				

					

				

			

		


		
		

			
💾 Backups
				

			
Saves your playtime (JSON + CSV) and achievements to a folder, daily or weekly, keeping the newest backups. Can also copy new screenshots there, sorted into a folder per game. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

			

				

				

					

						
						
						
					

					
					
					
					
					

				

				

			

		


		
		

			
🖼 Profile rotator
				

			
Changes your profile background, mini-profile background, avatar frame or animated avatar on a schedule, from the items you own. Click items below to rotate only through those.
